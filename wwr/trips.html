Family Float Adventure

Duration: 2 hours | Level: Beginner

Perfect for families and first-timers. Enjoy a gentle float down the scenic river with calm waters, wildlife spotting, and picnic stops. Our guides share stories about the local culture and history.

  • ✔ Suitable for ages 6+
  • ✔ Life vests and helmets included
  • ✔ Refreshments provided

Intermediate Rapids Run

Duration: 3 hours | Level: Intermediate

For those with some rafting experience. Navigate Class II–III rapids, work as a team, and feel the adrenaline rush. This trip offers the perfect balance of excitement and scenery.

  • ✔ Previous rafting experience recommended
  • ✔ All safety gear provided
  • ✔ Professional guide per raft

Extreme White Water Challenge

Duration: 4 hours | Level: Advanced

For the thrill-seekers! Tackle Class IV–V rapids with intense drops and powerful currents. This is the ultimate test of skill and courage. Must be physically fit and have previous rafting experience.

  • ✔ Minimum age 18
  • ✔ Advanced safety briefing included
  • ✔ Lunch and gear provided
